Plain Sight

Whatever you type on this page goes into a hidden buffer. The screen shows a document being written instead. Nothing you type leaves this page: there is no server, no account and no analytics, and the page never sends what you type anywhere.

  1. Peek. Press Ctrl + Shift + G to see what you actually wrote. Press it again, or type anything, to hide it. You can read and copy your text, not edit earlier parts of it.
  2. Copy. While peeking, press Ctrl + C to copy your real text to the clipboard.
  3. Clear. Press Esc to erase the hidden text and the screen. The page also clears itself after 10 minutes in a background tab. A saved copy on this device, if you turned that on below, stays and is locked.
  4. Save and open. Save downloads your text as a .sight file encrypted with AES-256-GCM, the key derived from your password with PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 310,000 rounds), unless you chose a folder or a plain .txt, .md or .docx download below, which save your words unencrypted. Open restores it and replaces what is on the page. There is no way to recover a forgotten password.

Reloading or closing the tab erases the hidden text unless you keep a copy on this device. The header belongs to the cover: its word count counts the text on screen, not your words.

Keep my text on this device (optional)

To come back to your text later: type your phrase on the blank page, then hold Ctrl, press E, then Enter. Nothing happens for a wrong phrase. There is no recovery for a forgotten one.

Where Save writes

Save downloads an encrypted .sight file.

Files in the folder are

With a folder chosen, Save writes a plain file named by the moment you press it, such as 2026-10-20 14-55-03.txt (or .md, or .docx for Word), straight into that folder. Plain means anyone who can open the file can read it, and the computer indexes, previews and backs it up like any document; a synced folder uploads it. Folder access exists in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers; Brave needs a setting turned on; Safari and Firefox download instead.

Super Stealth

This screen comes back when you click the ? at the bottom right the number of times set here, quickly, or when you press Ctrl + Shift + H, on a Mac too (Cmd + Shift + H also works where the browser leaves it to the page; Safari also uses it for its home page, so use Ctrl there). With the keyboard: Shift+Tab to the button (Option+Shift+Tab in Safari), then Enter the same number of times.
